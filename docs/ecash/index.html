4. Tip jar

A payment request to print or post. Anyone pays it from a Cashu wallet, and their wallet locks the tip to your signer, so a hacked phone or server holding the tips cannot spend them. Paste each tip into Receive: a locked one is kept as it is, an unlocked one is swapped into your vault.

What this page can and cannot do: it holds ecash that needs your signer's signature, so a copy of this page's storage cannot be spent. When you send, the sats you are paying pass through this page on their way out, and the signer shows you how many. The mint holds the bitcoin behind all of it. Format: ecash spend request.
