Simulator

The signer's own firmware, compiled to WebAssembly and drawn into the canvas below. Every screen, the real word list, the real derivation, a real signature. Nothing is installed and nothing leaves this tab.

A browser tab is not a signer. The keys made here live in a page anyone could be watching. Use it to learn the flow, then make the keys that matter on the device, offline.

Drag to draw. The cover screen is a game until you draw KISS across it.
