Simulator

The signer's own firmware, compiled to WebAssembly. Real words, real derivation, a real signature. Nothing installed, nothing leaves this tab.


A browser tab is not a signer. The keys made here live in a page anyone could be watching. Use it to learn the flow, then make the keys that matter on the device, offline.

Drag to draw. The cover screen is a game until you draw KISS across it. There is no camera behind CAMERA AND TAPS, so that one entropy source is filled in for you; the dice and the taps are yours.

Language

Chosen before the signer starts. After that it lives where it lives on the device: SETTINGS, inside the signer.

Show it a QR

Point a coordinator's QR at your webcam, or paste the payload.
