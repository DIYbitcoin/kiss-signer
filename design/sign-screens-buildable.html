01 main/kiss_sign.c  ·  companion drawing to Handoff 01

Every pixel here is one the device can actually draw.

The six redraws in KISS Signer Review.dc.html were drawn in a browser with a type scale the signer does not have: nine sizes where there are four, and a 600 weight that does not exist, because gen_fonts.sh builds every face from Montserrat Medium alone. This file redraws the two Sign screens under the real constraints, at true 800x480, using only faces the firmware can be given. Nothing here is scaled, and nothing is aspirational.

1b is the specification. 1c is the caution state. 1a is the zero cost fallback and is not the spec.

Why the address panel is the reason for the mono face

recipient panel, 2.5x

The beta7 changelog defines the compared characters as the four immediately after bc1q and the final four. Four plus four is the 8 the caption promises, so both runs are lit, and the caption cannot be attached to a single 10 character run the way it is today.

Handoff 01 says the mono face puts the lit runs "directly above" the comparison line. Drawn, it cannot: the two lines are at different sizes, so nothing columnar survives between them. What the fixed advance actually buys is better than that, and it is visible above.

What it buys, in order of how much it matters. One, a fixed advance is a tabular figure, so an amount occupies the same width on every screen it appears on. That is the request in PHASE 4 of the review, and it is about the hero and the footer figures more than the address. Two, fixed pitch makes the body scannable one character at a time, which is literally the task the caption sets. Three, both lit runs are four characters, so in mono they come out identical and their underlines match without hand tuning.

Be honest about the size of the third one. Measured below, the fallback misses by — on a 53px run. Visible when two underlines sit side by side, not damning on its own. The face earns its 16.7 KB on the first two reasons; the third is a bonus, and Handoff 01 should not lead with it.

The body is deliberately ungrouped, but not for the reason Handoff 01 gives. It claims grouping overflows the panel at about 465px. Measured here it is 437px against a 438px box, so it fits, by one pixel. One pixel is not a margin, and the arithmetic behind the 465 does not hold: 42 characters measure 353px, so 52 characters measure 437px, not 465px.

The decisive argument survives the correction and is the better one anyway. The address is 38 characters after bc1q, and 38 does not divide by four, so grouping splits the final four across a group boundary and breaks the very run the caption is pointing at.

1c Sign, cautions  ·  only y=150 to 270 differs from 1b

Glyph, ink and pill change. Geometry does not. The text column runs from the text's left edge at x=76 to the pill's at x=567, so — at wt_font14, measured off the row rather than quoted, and the longest English string in this drawing comes to —. German and Russian are the risk, not CJK. fitcheck is the gate, not this number.

HOLD TO SIGN never moves, never changes label, and never changes width. Today STR_C_I_UNDERSTAND occupies the sign button's position, so two taps in the same spot produce a signature nobody read. Here it keeps its coordinates and stays disabled ink WT_DIM until every row is acknowledged, then becomes wt_pill_primary. Rows are never taller than 56px: a taller row pushes the footer into the action bar, so if a locale cannot fit the column, shorten the string rather than wrap the row.

What it costs, visible above. The hero no longer dominates: at wt_font34 it is the same rung as the word SIGN, so the amount and the screen name carry equal weight, which is the wrong answer on a screen whose whole job is the amount. The address body loses fixed pitch, so "compare these 8" asks for a character by character read in a face that does not support one. The two compared runs come out — apart, so their underlines no longer match. And every figure on the screen goes proportional, which is the digits shifting column between screens that PHASE 4 of the review asked to fix.

What the three faces actually cost

The handoff estimated about 40 KB. Measured, it is 16.7 KB, roughly five thousandths of the headroom, and that is with the full printable ASCII set rather than the bech32 subset the handoff proposed. Subsetting further saves under 2 KB and buys a class of missing glyph bug, so take the ASCII set. Run tools/check_flash_budget.py after the real build and paste the PASS line regardless.

Corrections this drawing forced

wherewhat
content bottomFour values are in circulation: kiss_theme.h says 398, Handoff 01 draws the bar floor at 390, and the review README says 412 in two places. The work order makes the header authority, and the header's WT_ACTION_Y 404 and WT_ACTION_H 52 already match Handoff 01 exactly. Every frame here uses 398, bar 398 to 480, pills 404 to 456. Fix the two documents.
caution heroThe review drops the caution hero to 40px. There is no 40 rung. Handoff 01 is right that the hero is unchanged, and this drawing keeps it at 48, which also stops the two Sign states looking like two screens.
fingerprint caseHandoff 01 subsets the mono faces to "digits, lowercase ASCII and the bech32 charset", but the fingerprint is uppercase hex (EC5A4595) and derivation paths need m / '. Full printable ASCII costs under 2 KB more. Take it.
"directly above"Handoff 01 justifies the mono face by saying it puts the two lit spans at a fixed character width directly above the comparison line. The two lines are at different sizes, so no column can survive between them, and the drawing shows it does not. The real justification, in order, is tabular figures for the amounts, then a body the user can scan one character at a time, then matched run widths. Measured, that last one is worth —, so it should not be the headline. Rewrite the paragraph; the conclusion is unchanged.
grouped widthHandoff 01 says the grouped address needs about 465px and therefore wraps. Measured, it is 437px against a 438px box, so it fits by one pixel. The 465 came from bad arithmetic, not from a drawing. Keep the address ungrouped, but say so for the right reason: 38 does not divide by four, so grouping splits the lit tail across a group boundary. Also amend the sentence claiming this "was drawn both ways and measured", because it now has been.
caution columnHandoff 01 says the column runs to "the pill's left edge at x=593" and is therefore 515px. Its own row spec puts the pill at relative x=543 inside a row at x=24, which is absolute 567, so the column is 491px. Both numbers cannot be right and the drawing uses the one the coordinates produce. Fix the 593 and the 515.
caution widthHandoff 01 estimates the longest English caution at about 357px. Measured at wt_font14 it is —, so headroom against the corrected 491px column is about 1.15x, not the 1.44x claimed. It still passes, but German and Russian have far less room than the handoff implies, and this is now the tightest thing on either screen.
flash estimateAbout 40 KB, measured at 16.7 KB. Corrected above.
network chipUnchanged and confirmed: network state lives in the footer cell at x=294, which exists in both mainnet and testnet at the same coordinates, so testnet adds no new element. Defect 01 closes by deletion, not relocation.
